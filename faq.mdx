## Booking and Cancellation Policies

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="How do I book a trip with TWG?" default-open="true">

To book a trip, visit our [travel offers page](https://www.twerenbold.ch/#) and select your desired journey. Choose dates, enter passenger details, and complete payment. You'll receive a confirmation email with all details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Trip">
    Browse busreisen, flug & bus, or other categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    Provide contact information and special requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Payment">
    Review and pay securely.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What is your cancellation policy?">

Cancellations are free up to 30 days before departure. Between 29-15 days, a 20% fee applies. Within 14 days, full payment is due. Contact support for refunds.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Travel insurance is recommended to cover unexpected cancellations.
</Callout>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I modify my booking?">

Yes, changes to dates or passengers are possible up to 48 hours before departure, subject to availability and fees. Log in to your account or contact us.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Prices, Discounts, and Payments

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="How are prices calculated?" default-open="true">

Prices include transportation, accommodation, and meals as specified. They vary by season, group size, and trip type. Check for early bird discounts.

| Trip Type     | Base Price (CHF) | Includes          |
|---------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Busreisen     | 250+             | Bus, hotel        |
| Flug & Bus    | 500+             | Flight, bus, hotel|
| Tagesausflüge | 50+              | Day trip only     |

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What payment methods do you accept?">

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Credit Card" icon="credit-card">
    Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Secure 3D Secure processing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bank Transfer" icon="banknote">
    Pay via TWINT or direct transfer. Details in confirmation email.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Installments" icon="calendar">
    Available for trips over CHF 500. 50% upfront, rest before departure.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Are there discounts available?">

Seniors (65+), children (under 12), and groups of 10+ get 10-20% off. Early bookings save up to 15%. Apply codes at checkout.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Travel Information and Requirements

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="What travel documents do I need?" default-open="true">

Valid passport or ID for EU trips. For international, check visa requirements. We provide a checklist in your booking confirmation.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What about COVID-19 or health requirements?">

Follow current regulations. Proof of vaccination or tests may be required. Check our [Reiseinfos](https://www.twerenbold.ch/rund-ums-reisen/reiseinfos) page before travel.

<Callout kind="info">
  Regulations change frequently. Verify 72 hours prior to departure.
</Callout>

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Are the buses accessible?">

Most buses have wheelchair access. Notify us at booking for assistance. Comfortable seating and Wi-Fi on long trips.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>
