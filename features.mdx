## Overview

TWG offers a wide range of travel experiences tailored to your preferences, from comfortable bus journeys to themed adventures. You can explore bus trips, flight and bus combinations, music tours, bike excursions, group travels, and seasonal specials like Christmas markets. All options prioritize excellent value with the slogan: Reisen in guter Gesellschaft – bester Preis-/Leistung.

## Travel Categories

Explore detailed options through these popular categories. Each provides unique itineraries, accommodations, and inclusions.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Busreisen & Flug & Bus" icon="route">
    Busreisen feature comfortable, air-conditioned coaches with onboard amenities. Flug & Bus packages integrate short flights for longer distances, saving time and costs.
    
    Key benefits:
    
    - Daily departures from major cities
    - Hotel stays with breakfast
    - Expert local guides
    
    <Callout kind="tip">
      Book early for peak seasons to secure prime seats.
    </Callout>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Musikreisen & Veloreisen" icon="music">
    Musikreisen take you to concerts and festivals in stunning venues. Veloreisen offer guided bike tours with e-bike rentals and luggage transport.
    
    ```
    Sample Itinerary:
    Day 1: Arrival & bike fitting
    Day 2-5: Scenic rides (40-60km/day)
    Day 6: Festival evening
    ```
    
    Rentals include helmets and repair kits.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Booking Your Trip

Follow these steps to reserve your adventure quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search Trips" icon="search">
    Use the search bar to filter by type, date, and destination. Refine with price sliders and group size.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Details" icon="list">
    Check inclusions like meals, transfers, and insurance. View photos and customer ratings.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Book & Pay" icon="credit-card">
    Select passengers, apply discounts, and complete payment. Receive instant confirmation via email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
