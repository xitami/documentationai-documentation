## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">
Before starting, ensure you have:
- A valid email address for account verification
- Payment method ready (credit card or bank transfer)
- Internet connection for browsing trips
</Callout>

## Create an Account

Follow these steps to sign up and log in to your TWG account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Navigate to `https://dashboard.example.com` and click **Sign Up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details" icon="user">
    Provide your email, create a strong password, and accept the terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification link and click it to activate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the dashboard, enter your credentials, and access your profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Browse and Search Trips

Discover trips like bus tours, music journeys, or winter getaways using the search tools.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web" icon="monitor">
    Use the search bar at the top: enter keywords like "Weihnachtsmärkte" or filter by "Busreisen".
    
    Select dates and destinations from the dropdowns.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile App" icon="smartphone">
    Open the app, tap the magnifying glass icon, and apply filters for "Veloreisen" or "Flussreisen".
    
    Save favorites for quick access later.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
Pro tip: Use advanced filters for group sizes, prices, or themes to find the best match quickly.
</Callout>

## Book Your First Trip

Once you find a trip, proceed to booking.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Trip" icon="map-pin">
    Click on a trip like "Tagesausflüge" to view details, itinerary, and pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Cart" icon="shopping-cart">
    Choose dates, number of travelers, and any add-ons like car rental.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkout" icon="credit-card">
    Review your selection, enter passenger details, and proceed to payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Payment" icon="check-circle">
    Complete payment and receive confirmation email with voucher and details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For developers integrating bookings, here's a simple API example:

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,cURL">
  ```javascript
  const response = await fetch('https://api.example.com/v1/trips/search', {
    method: 'POST',
    headers: { 'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_TOKEN', 'Content-Type': 'application/json' },
    body: JSON.stringify({
      query: 'Busreisen',
      dates: { start: '2024-12-01', end: '2024-12-10' }
    })
  });
  const trips = await response.json();
  console.log(trips);
  ```
  ```bash
  curl -X POST https://api.example.com/v1/trips/search \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{
      "query": "Busreisen",
      "dates": { "start": "2024-12-01", "end": "2024-12-10" }
    }'
  ```
</CodeGroup>
